### Projects

**[PowerCo churn prediction](https://github.com/tamanna-srivastava/powerco-churn-prediction)** · Python, scikit-learn

Predicting which business customers of a gas and electricity utility will leave, from the BCG Data Science job simulation. The client suspected price was the cause; the analysis points to tenure instead, with customers in their first five years churning at 11.5% against 7% for those with five to ten years. Includes threshold tuning for a heavily imbalanced target.

**[Telecoms churn prediction](https://github.com/tamanna-srivastava/telecoms-customer-churn-prediction)** · R, R Markdown

Identifying likely churners among 20,000 telecoms customers using decision trees, logistic regression, KNN and k-means clustering. Customers who regularly exceed their call allowance are the highest-risk group, churning at about 79% against a 49% base rate.
